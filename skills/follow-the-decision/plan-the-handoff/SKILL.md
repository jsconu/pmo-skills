# Plan the Handoff

Handoffs fail on one confusion: assigned is not the same as owned. Something with a name next to it in a tracker may never have been accepted by that person. Keep the three states apart.

## The three states

- **Assigned**: someone's name is on it.
- **Acknowledged**: that person accepted it, in their own words or by acting on it. Note the evidence.
- **Done**: the work is finished, shown by evidence, not by someone saying it's nearly there.

Reassigning something resets it to assigned. The new owner has to acknowledge it.

## Method

1. List every open item from the material: follow-ups, reviews, actions, promised replies.
2. Classify each one, with the evidence.
3. For anything moving to a new owner, mark it **needs acknowledgement** and draft the one-line ask.
4. Flag items with no owner, no date, or a date already passed.
5. Note any context the new owner needs that only the person leaving knows. Ask the user for it rather than guessing.

Don't close or send anything. The user decides.

## Output

```
**Handoff from <name> to <name>, <date>**

| Item | Current owner | New owner | State | Due | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ... | <name> | <name> | Assigned / Acknowledged / Done / Needs acknowledgement | <date or "No date"> | "<quote>" |

**Needs an owner:** ...
**Overdue:** ...
**Asks to send:**
- To <new owner>: "Can you confirm you're taking over <item>, due <date>?"

**Context only <leaving person> has:** <questions to ask them before they go>
```
