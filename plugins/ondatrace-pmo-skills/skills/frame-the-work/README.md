# Frame the work

Starting something new: the sponsor, the charter, the project list, the backlog.

| Skill | What it does | Claude upload |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[draft-the-charter](./draft-the-charter/SKILL.md)** | Turn kickoff notes, a sponsor interview or a request email into a project charter that labels every field as confirmed, inferred or unconfirmed and lists who can close each gap. | [Download](https://github.com/jsconu/pmo-skills/releases/latest/download/draft-the-charter.zip) |
| **[find-the-projects](./find-the-projects/SKILL.md)** | Sort a messy batch of notes, emails, tickets or chat exports into the candidate projects and initiatives they describe, with aliases and the boundaries that need a PMO lead to confirm. | [Download](https://github.com/jsconu/pmo-skills/releases/latest/download/find-the-projects.zip) |
| **[interview-the-sponsor](./interview-the-sponsor/SKILL.md)** | Interview a sponsor or requester about a new project, one question at a time, until the problem, authority, scope and success criteria are clear enough to charter. | [Download](https://github.com/jsconu/pmo-skills/releases/latest/download/interview-the-sponsor.zip) |
| **[prioritize-the-backlog](./prioritize-the-backlog/SKILL.md)** | Put a backlog or list of candidate work into a reasoned order while keeping pinned items and fixed commitments exactly where they are, and show what moved and why. | [Download](https://github.com/jsconu/pmo-skills/releases/latest/download/prioritize-the-backlog.zip) |
