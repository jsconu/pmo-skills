# Follow the decision

After the decision: did it work, did it land, who owns what, and are people ready.

| Skill | What it does | Claude upload |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[check-the-decision](./check-the-decision/SKILL.md)** | Take a past decision, its expected result and fresh evidence, and say whether the intervention worked, what is still unknown and when to check again. | [Download](https://github.com/jsconu/pmo-skills/releases/latest/download/check-the-decision.zip) |
| **[plan-change-adoption](./plan-change-adoption/SKILL.md)** | For a delivery change (a new system, process, date or scope change) and the groups it affects, propose the communications, training and adoption checks needed, labelled as recommendations. | [Download](https://github.com/jsconu/pmo-skills/releases/latest/download/plan-change-adoption.zip) |
| **[plan-the-handoff](./plan-the-handoff/SKILL.md)** | Turn open reviews, follow-ups, owners and dates into a handoff that separates work that is assigned, work that is acknowledged by its owner and work that is actually done. | [Download](https://github.com/jsconu/pmo-skills/releases/latest/download/plan-the-handoff.zip) |
| **[trace-a-decision](./trace-a-decision/SKILL.md)** | Take an approved change and the team artifacts that should reflect it (plans, tickets, schedules, customer commitments, messages) and show where the decision appears, where the evidence still shows the old position, and who should verify adoption. | [Download](https://github.com/jsconu/pmo-skills/releases/latest/download/trace-a-decision.zip) |
