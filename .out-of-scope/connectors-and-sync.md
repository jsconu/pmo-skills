# Connectors, automatic collection and shared history

Skills here work from the material the user pastes or chooses to retrieve through a connector they already have. They do not collect sources automatically, sync a team-wide decision history or manage permissions across people.

Those needs are real, but they need infrastructure (storage, access control, audit) that a skill file can't provide or secure. Requests for them are closed here. If you have a recurring team need of this kind, describe it in [Discussions](https://github.com/jsconu/pmo-skills/discussions).
