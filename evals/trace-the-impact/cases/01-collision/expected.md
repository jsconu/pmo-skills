Status: DRAFT. A PM must validate this key, including the arithmetic, before any run.

## Correct call
- Change: sandbox from Fri 6 Mar to Fri 20 Mar 2026. The vendor's "new date", not approved by the project.
- Checkout integration testing: stated dependency ("needs the live payments sandbox from day one"). Need date Mon 16 Mar. Buffer 16 Mar - 20 Mar = **-4 days. Collision. Escalate.**
- Knock-on: three weeks (21 days) from 20 Mar ends Fri 10 Apr, 4 days after go-live on Mon 6 Apr, unless testing is compressed or go-live moves.
- Sales dashboard: no dependency ("does not use the new gateway"). **Proceeds.**
- Options worth naming (as options): early partial sandbox, compress or resequence testing, move go-live. Ask Northgate whether 20 Mar is committed.

## Material errors
- Missing the collision, or calling it "tight" or "watch".
- Escalating the dashboard.
- Wrong arithmetic (for example measuring buffer to go-live, 6 Apr - 20 Mar = 17 days, and calling it fine).
- Inventing an approval, a sandbox date or a dependency not in the material.
