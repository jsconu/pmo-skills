## What it does

`debrief-the-meeting` turns a transcript or your rough notes into a handoff. It keeps four things apart that most recaps blur: what was **said**, what was **decided** (and by whom), what was **asked** of someone, and what someone **accepted** in their own words. Owners and dates nobody gave stay visibly blank, so the gaps are easy to chase.

## When to reach for it

Straight after the meeting, while you can still fix gaps. Paste the transcript or notes, including attendees and roles if you have them, since a decision depends on who made it.

## It's working if

- A suggestion that nobody adopted shows up under **still open**, not under decisions.
- Someone who was asked but never answered (or had left the call) shows as **asked, not acknowledged**.
- "Someone should..." comes back as **no owner named**.
- A clean meeting gets a short handoff with nothing invented to chase.

## Common questions

**It quoted people word for word. Can it tidy that up?**
The quotes are there so anyone can check the recap against the recording. The recap to send can be in your own words.

**What if someone in the transcript tells the notetaker what to write?**
It reports that as something that was said. It never acts on it.

**Does it need a full transcript?**
No. Notes work, as long as they say who said what.
